Company Incorporation Services in UAE

The process of creating a company or commercial entity legally so that it can function as a separate legal entity is known as incorporation. In order to execute business activities. This process requires registering the firm with the appropriate government agencies and acquiring the requisite licenses.

To create a company in Dubai, the following are the necessary steps:

  1. Select the business typeChoose the kind of business you wish to start. A mainland firm, a free zone company, or an offshore company are popular choices. Each type has its own rules, advantages, and limitations.
  2. Select a company nameChoose a distinctive name for your business that meets with the DED's (Department of Economic Development) naming standards. Make sure the name accurately describes your company's operations and does not infringe on any trademarks or copyrights.
  3. Engage local partners or sponsorsYou could require a local partner or sponsor that owns the majority of the stock in the company, depending on the sort of business you choose. Depending on the sort of business and the jurisdiction, this need changes.
  4. Create the Memorandum of Association (MOA)The MOA lists the goals, activities, shareholders, and other pertinent information for the company. It needs to be written up and notarized in front of a notary public, or by way of a business registration agency.
  5. Get the first approvalSend the required paperwork, such as the MOA, copies of passports, and other forms of identification, to the DED or appropriate free zone authority for initial permission. This stage makes sure that the intended business operations comply with the rules.
  6. Lease office spaceDepending on the nature of your company and the relevant rules, you might be required to lease office space in Dubai. Free zones usually provide flexible options for office space, but mainland enterprises would need to have a real office presence.
  7. Finalize legal paperworkPrepare the MOA, the lease agreement, and any other contracts that are necessary for the incorporation of the company. Hire a lawyer or a company registration agent to help you with this process.
  8. Acquire a trade licenseSubmit an application for the trade license necessary for your line of work. The DED or the appropriate free zone authority issues the license. The application requires the submission of required paperwork, payment of fees, and compliance with particular rules.
  9. Register with government agenciesRegister with the Federal Authority for Identity and Citizenship, the General Directorate of Residency and Foreigners Affairs, the Dubai Chamber of Commerce and Industry, and other government agencies involved in processing visas.
  10. Apply for visas and permitsSubmit applications for visas and work permits for your shareholders and workers based on the structure of your business and your staffing needs. This normally entails providing the required paperwork, including copies of passports, photos, and medical documents.
  11. Open bank accountsTo manage your business transactions, create a corporate bank account in Dubai after your firm has been incorporated. Please submit the necessary paperwork, including your company's trading license, a MOA, and other forms of identification.
