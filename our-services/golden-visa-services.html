Golden Visa Services in Dubai, UAE

The "Golden Visa" is a residency program introduced by the United Arab Emirates (UAE), including Dubai, to attract foreign investors, entrepreneurs, talented professionals, and their families to live and work in the country. It offers long-term residency permits with a range of benefits to eligible individuals. We are specialized for Golden Visa services in dubai, UAE.

The Golden Visa provides an extended residency period beyond the usual tourist or employment visas. It allows individuals to live and work in the UAE for a longer duration, typically ranging from 5 to 10 years, and can be renewed. The visa also offers various benefits, such as:

  • ResidencyHolders of the Golden Visa can reside in the UAE for an extended period, providing stability and security for themselves and their families.
  • Business OpportunitiesGolden Visa holders can establish and own businesses in the UAE without the need for a local sponsor or partner.
  • EducationThe visa facilitates access to educational institutions in the UAE for the visa holder and their dependents.
  • Health CareGolden Visa holders can access the healthcare system in the UAE, including government and private healthcare facilities.
  • SponsorshipThe visa allows Golden Visa holders to sponsor their immediate family members, including spouse and children, enabling them to reside in the UAE.

Formation of Golden Visa Services in Dubai

An investor in public investments

  1. Bringing a letter from an investment fund approved in the UAE stating that there is a deposit of 2 million dirhams
  2. Or bringing a valid commercial or industrial license with the company’s incorporation contract with the investor so that the investor's capital is not less than 2 million dirhams.
  3. Investing in a new or existing company as a partner with shares of a minimum of AED 2 million, not financed by a loan.
  4. Or bring a letter from the Federal Tax Authority stating that he is the owner of a company that pays the government no less than 250 thousand dirhams annually. On the other hand, bring a letter from the Federal Tax Authority stating that he is a partner in an establishment that pays the tax to the government and whose share in the tax is no less than 250 thousand dirhams annually.
  5. Passport copy
  6. Visa copy
  7. Personal photo
  8. Valid medical insurance
  9. Medical test

  4. Visa copy
