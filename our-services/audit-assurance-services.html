Audit & Assurance Services in UAE

We are providing Audit and assurance services in Dubai, UAE by qualified auditors to assess an independent opinion on the accuracy and reliability of financial statements and other financial information of an organization.

Here are some key components of audit and assurance services:

  • Financial Statement AuditThis is the most common type of audit service. It involves a thorough examination of an organization's financial statements, including the balance sheet, income statement, and cash flow statement. The auditor assesses whether the financial statements are prepared in accordance with relevant accounting standards, present a true and fair view of the organization's financial position, and disclose all material information.
  • Compliance AuditCompliance audits focus on evaluating an organization's adherence to applicable laws, regulations, and industry standards. The auditor assesses whether the organization has complied with legal and regulatory requirements and determines any areas of non-compliance that need attention.
  • Internal AuditInternal audit services are provided by auditors employed within an organization or outsourced to an external audit firm. Internal auditors assess the effectiveness and efficiency of internal controls, risk management processes, and governance structures within the organization. They provide recommendations for improving operations, mitigating risks, and enhancing overall organizational performance.
  • IT AuditIT audit services involve the evaluation of an organization's IT systems, infrastructure, and controls. The auditor assesses the reliability and security of IT systems, the effectiveness of data management processes, and the organization's ability to protect sensitive information from unauthorized access or cyber threats.
  • Review EngagementsReview engagements provide a limited level of assurance on financial statements. The auditor performs analytical procedures and inquiries to determine if the financial statements are plausible and free from material misstatements. However, the level of assurance provided in a review engagement is lower compared to a full financial statement audit.
  • Assurance on Non-Financial InformationBesides financial information, assurance services can also be provided on non-financial information. This can include sustainability reports, corporate social responsibility (CSR) disclosures, or other non-financial performance indicators. The auditor evaluates the reliability and accuracy of such information and provides an independent opinion or assurance.

It's important to note that audit and assurance services are typically conducted in accordance with relevant auditing standards, professional ethics, and regulatory requirements. The specific scope and nature of these services may vary depending on the nature of the engagement and the needs of the organization.
