VAT & Corporate Tax Filing Services in UAE

We have Corporate Tax services as well as VAT services. In the United Arab Emirates (UAE), businesses are required to comply with certain tax regulations, including VAT & corporate tax in Dubai, UAE. To ensure proper compliance and avoid penalties, many businesses seek the assistance of tax filing services.

VAT Filing Services:

Value Added Tax (VAT) was introduced in the UAE on January 1, 2018. It is a consumption tax imposed on the supply of goods and services. VAT Services in Dubai, UAE assist businesses in managing their VAT-related obligations. These services typically include:

  • VAT RegistrationHelping businesses with the initial VAT registration process and obtaining a Tax Registration Number (TRN) from the Federal Tax Authority (FTA).
  • VAT ReturnsPreparing and filing VAT returns on a quarterly basis, including calculating the VAT liability or refund.
  • Compliance ReviewConducting periodic reviews of financial records and transactions to ensure VAT compliance.
  • Record-KeepingAssisting businesses in maintaining proper records and documentation to support VAT filings.
  • VAT AdvisoryProviding guidance and advice on VAT matters, such as the applicability of VAT on specific transactions or industries.

Corporate Tax Filing Services:

Federal corporate tax applies in the UAE to financial years starting on or after 1 June 2023. Taxable income up to AED 375,000 is taxed at 0%, and the portion above AED 375,000 is taxed at 9%. A free zone person that meets the conditions of a Qualifying Free Zone Person can benefit from a 0% rate on its qualifying income, while its other income is taxed at 9%. All taxable persons, including free zone persons, must register with the Federal Tax Authority for corporate tax and obtain a registration number. Corporate tax filing services in the UAE primarily focus on assisting businesses with these requirements, which may include:

  • Corporate Tax RegistrationHelping businesses, including free zone companies, register for corporate tax with the Federal Tax Authority (FTA) and obtain a corporate tax registration number.
  • Corporate Tax AssessmentsDetermining whether a business is subject to corporate tax, whether it can qualify as a Qualifying Free Zone Person, and how its income is treated based on its activities and location.
  • Corporate Tax ReturnsPreparing the corporate tax computation and filing the annual corporate tax return.
  • Tax PlanningAdvising businesses on tax-efficient strategies and structures to minimize tax liabilities.
  • Tax OptimizationIdentifying potential tax incentives, exemptions, or deductions that a business may be eligible for.
  • Tax RepresentationRepresenting businesses in dealings with the tax authorities, if required.

It's important to note that tax regulations can change over time, and businesses should consult with professional advisors or tax filing services to ensure compliance with the latest requirements and obligations in the UAE.
